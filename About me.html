I make complex products simple enough to trust.

Product and UX designer based in Mumbai, open across domains. Strength is complex products where trust matters — banking, AI tools, and consumer apps among them.

I currently design SME and corporate banking at Intellect Design: payments, approvals, onboarding, and the design system that keeps those surfaces consistent on web and mobile. Before that, and alongside it, I designed Daughters, an iPhone product whose first session had to complete a prayer or there was nothing to launch.

The work I want is the same in every domain. Find the job the person actually came to do. Cut everything that sits in front of it. Leave a product that still feels like one thing when the screens multiply.
